One agent works these in order, reading this change (proposal, design,
specs) and building on the tasks before each. The statement is read at
blueprints 31f9f1c: 52, 57 and 89 as amended there, and C.4's
`[kinds.train]` example with its paragraph on defaults. core.31, with

`crates/weir-kinds/tests/fixtures/`, and tests reach no file outside

passing what follows `--` to `cargo test`.
