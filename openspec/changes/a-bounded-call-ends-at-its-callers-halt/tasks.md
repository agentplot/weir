# Tasks

One agent works these in order, reading this change (proposal, design,
specs) and building on the tasks before each. Record 0008's amendment of
2026-11-12 and core.53 are on this branch, from weir's main at 41cd694;
the statement's 69 and C.3 are read at blueprints ad3a1ae and hold as
written. No source or test of a crate names a tool of the family
(core.2), no test reaches a network, and a halt is set mid-call from a
thread the test makes with `std::thread::scope` (design 9). A test named
for a scenario holds that scenario and keeps its name, in snake case.
Each check runs one crate's tests, one of its test binaries or one test,
through that crate's moon task, moon passing what follows `--` to
`cargo test`. The task groups are headings for reading. The verify stage
runs the repository's whole checks once every task is done.
